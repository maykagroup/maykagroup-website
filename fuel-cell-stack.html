Hydrogen Fuel Cell Stack

Mayka Group manufactures advanced hydrogen fuel cell stacks for transportation, marine and stationary power applications.

Our PEM fuel cell stacks provide reliable performance, high efficiency and long operating lifetime for global clean energy solutions.

PEM Fuel Cell Stack Technology

Our hydrogen fuel cell stacks are developed based on advanced Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM) fuel cell technology.

The products are designed for high power density, stable operation and excellent durability under different operating conditions.

  • High power density design
  • Excellent fuel efficiency
  • Long service lifetime
  • Reliable performance in demanding environments
